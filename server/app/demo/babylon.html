Babylon.js — Realtime Demo

3D version of the top-down demo. Move with WASD or arrow keys (tank controls: W/S forward/back, A/D turn), B for Samba, orbit with the mouse. Walk over a gem to collect it. Gems are item-state — updates spawn them, collections collect them. Every player gets a floating name bar + health bar above their head. No timers anywhere: movement is keydown/keyup + render loop, disconnect is enforced by the server on the next packet.

Disconnect rule: the server stores last_seen per client and disconnectionTime from your config (default 60s). On every inbound packet from any client it sweeps expired clients and evicts them with reason: "timeout". There is no timer loop. If nobody sends a packet, an idle client stays listed until the next packet arrives. Your own late packet is rejected (403 disconnected: timeout) instead of reviving you. Keep-alive is just another packet: this demo sends character-state while moving and /heartbeat from the render loop when idle.
